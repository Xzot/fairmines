How can every board be guess-free?

When you make your first tap, the app builds a board around it and a solver plays it the way a careful person would, using only what the numbers prove. If it ever reaches a spot that would need a guess, it moves a mine and checks again. Only boards it can finish from start to end with logic are shown to you.

How do I play?

  • Tap a cell to open it. Touch and hold to place a flag. You can change the hold time in Settings.
  • Prefer one hand? Switch the control under the board from Open to Flag, and taps place flags instead.
  • Tap an open number that already has all its flags around it to open the rest of its neighbors.
  • Pinch to zoom and drag to move around big boards. Double-tap the empty space around the board to zoom in or out.

How do hints work?

A hint highlights the numbers that matter and the cell they decide, then explains why in plain words, for example "This 1 already touches a flagged mine, so its other closed neighbors are safe." Free players get 3 hints a day and can watch a short ad for 3 more. Pro hints are unlimited. Games where you used a hint don't count for best times or leaderboards.

What is the Daily Board?

One Intermediate board a day, the same for everyone. It starts with an area already open, and the clock starts on your first move. Solve it each day to grow your streak. A new board appears at midnight in your time zone.

How do I add the widgets?

Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, tap the widget area and choose Fair Mines.

Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Fair Mines and pick a size.

What does Pro include?

The game is free with ads. Fair Mines Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and adds unlimited hints, the Retro, Paper and Night themes, detailed statistics and Custom boards up to 50×50. It works with Family Sharing.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Can I change my ad privacy choices?

If you're in a region where consent is required, open Settings → Ad privacy choices. Tracking permission can be changed anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
